The Sovereign Ledger Protocol

Aethelgard replaces the salary model with Direct Equity. In this collective, every verified hour of labor is logged as a permanent, non-dilutable share of the system's total surplus. Hour for hour, you own what you build.

01 // Maritime Synthesis

Our autonomous fleets utilize solar-thermal processing to harvest PET and HDPE from oceanic gyres, converting global waste into graphene-infused structural blocks for deep-sea and terrestrial infrastructure.

02 // Lunar Logistics

03 // Surplus Distribution
