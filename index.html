// INITIATIVE

AETHELGARD // GLOBAL

Imagine a world. A world in unison. A world that works together. Maybe even a world at peace. A world giving effort to equalize pay and create a machine that works for all. A business worked for and owned by the workforce. One major currency for the working class people of the planet. A business that has no ties and no buy-ins from any outsiders. A business for the people and by the people. Where would the funds come from to start such a venture? That is the root of all evil. The ones in power do not want to share that power. Our populations are working for the bottom fraction of a percent to share amongst ourselves. Companies have become so full of greed, they do not even care to help the people that are working for them. A company built by an individual can remain owned by said individual. If a company were built from a collective, it could thrive for the collective. This movement would require major cooperation from the powers that be. Whether billionaires or governments, they have the world’s purse. If a shift in wealth occurred, it would have to come from the top down. The whole “idea” of the top helping the bottom. The bottom, then able to build up to the middle, and have the world’s power flipped to the people. Let the markets start being owned by a workforce of the planet. A global effort to overcome the desperations of mankind and turn the world green. Let the planet thrive as well as the people. We can all fix a broken planet. I have a vision of an effort towards cleaning our environment. Building affordable housing for all who need it. Building wealth for the bottom up. If the floor raises, so does the ceiling. If every worker were to have a say in the sovereign community, if they had shares only that can be earned with labor, it would be a just system. Every hour worked, getting a share in the collective. Starting with our beautiful oceans. We have so many plastics floating around in our waters and being buried in our lands. These plastics could be made into a million different things. The topmost to build housing. If we were to create building blocks out of recycled plastics, we could build housing around the entire world. We purify these plastics and create HDPE material (High-Density Polyethylene), and create the molds of the many uses. To turn the world’s trash into houses would be amazing. Even the roof shingles could be pressed out of our forge. We build sheets, blocks, shingles, and any other type of mold needed to construct an entire house. What if we could build in space? An entire space station made of Earth’s trash? Worker built. Worker owned. A global collective that could change the entire future of mankind. With the correct group of people that have enough stature to actually sway any type of powers that be, momentous things could happen. This could truly be the single greatest thing mankind has ever accomplished.
