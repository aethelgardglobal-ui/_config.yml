Project Briefing: The Sovereign Shift

Subject: Integration of Labor and Ownership via the Aethelgard Protocol.

01 // The Origin Mission

Aethelgard was founded on the rejection of the "Rent-Seeker" economy. Traditional industrial models extract value from the worker to pay the shareholder. In our model, the worker is the shareholder.

We are tackling the two greatest frontiers of resource acquisition: the oceanic waste gyres and the lunar regolith. These are not just environmental or scientific goals; they are the fuel for a new, independent economy.

02 // Hour-for-Hour Shares

The core of the Sovereign Ledger is the 1:1 Labor-to-Equity ratio. We have eliminated complex vesting schedules and corporate stock options in favor of a mathematical constant:

1 HR LABOR = 1 SOVEREIGN SHARE

Shares are non-dilutable and issued instantly upon verification of task completion within the Ledger.

Whether you are an engineer optimizing a depolymerization reactor or a technician maintaining a lunar extractor, your time is your capital. 60% of all surplus generated by Aethelgard assets is distributed back to these shares monthly.
