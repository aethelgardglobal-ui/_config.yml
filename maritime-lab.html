Maritime Synthesis

Thermal depolymerization of oceanic polymer waste into Graphene-Structural Blocks (GSB).

The Process

01 Collection: Harvesting HDPE/PET waste from high-density oceanic gyres.

02 Pyrolysis: Solar-thermal decomposition at $700^\circ C$ in anaerobic chambers.

03 Synthesis: Molecular graphene layering to achieve superior structural lattice integrity.
