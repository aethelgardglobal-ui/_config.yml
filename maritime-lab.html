LAB_REF: MS-BLOCK-V1
PROJECT: BLUE VANGUARD

Maritime Synthesis Lab

Direct thermal depolymerization of oceanic polymer waste into high-density graphene structural blocks.

The Synthesis Process

01 Collection: Autonomous maritime swarms identify and harvest PET/HDPE concentrations in oceanic gyres.
02 Thermal Processing: Concentrated solar-thermal arrays heat waste to 450°C in oxygen-free chambers.
03 Graphene Infusion: Liquid carbon-black is refined into graphene sheets and layered into the polymer lattice.
04 Curing: Vacuum-pressed molds create interlocking G-Blocks for deep-sea or lunar habitats.
